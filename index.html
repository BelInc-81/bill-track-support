Simple Bill Tracker Support

Simple Bill Tracker helps you track recurring and one-time bills against your paychecks, so you always know what's covered and what's left over.

Where is my data stored?

Everything you enter — bills, deposits, balances, and what's marked paid — is saved only on your device. Simple Bill Tracker has no account system and no servers, so nothing is ever uploaded anywhere.

I deleted the app and my bills are gone

Because your data lives only on your device, uninstalling Simple Bill Tracker permanently erases everything you entered — there's currently no cloud backup or restore. If you're planning to delete the app temporarily (e.g., to free up space), export or note down your bills first, as reinstalling will start fresh.

How do I mark a bill as paid?

Tap the circle next to a bill on the Bills or Overview tab to toggle it paid for the current month.

How do I track an outstanding balance (like a credit card)?

When adding or editing a bill, enable "Has Outstanding Balance" and set a starting amount. A progress bar will appear on that bill showing how much you've paid off; tap "Pay ›" to log a payment.

How do I add income from multiple paychecks?

Add each paycheck as a separate deposit (recurring or one-time) in the Deposits tab. The Overview tab groups your bills under whichever paycheck is set to cover them.
